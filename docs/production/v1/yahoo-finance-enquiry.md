## Final enquiry

**Subject: Yahoo Finance API access and data retention for a private personal pilot**

Hello,

I am developing Keystone, a financial research and monitoring application. This
enquiry concerns its next private personal evaluation: one user, local reports,
no employer/client use and no external distribution. I do not have a separate
financial-data licence.

The development integration uses the third-party Python library yfinance. Could
you confirm whether this access method is permitted for the proposed pilot under
applicable terms, or identify the official API, agreement and contact we should use?

The intended inputs are annual financial statements, historical prices and
corporate actions, share information, analyst estimates/targets, earnings dates
and news headlines or summaries. Initial companies are Stadler Rail on SIX in
CHF, ASML on Euronext Amsterdam and NVIDIA in the US.

The application calculates ratios and valuations and retains raw inputs,
provenance and derived HTML/JSON reports for replay. Please clarify permitted
retention, local and encrypted recovery copies, attribution, publisher/exchange
restrictions and handling after cancellation or access termination. Please assess
news text separately from numeric data.

If a suitable offering is available, please provide its coverage, usage limits,
pricing and governing terms. External AI processing is excluded from this pilot
request; any such use would require separate assessment. Future shared or
commercial deployments would also need a separate agreement assessment.

Please route this request to the appropriate team if necessary. This is an
information request, not a subscription order or acceptance of charges.

Thank you.
