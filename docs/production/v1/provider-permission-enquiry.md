## Subject

API entitlement and retained-data rights for a private personal analytics pilot

## Enquiry text

Hello,

I am evaluating Keystone, a local financial research and monitoring application,
solely in my personal capacity. There is one user, no employer or client use, no
commercial service and no external sharing of data or reports. I do not currently
hold a financial-data licence. Please confirm whether personal software evaluation
qualifies for your proposed plan, rather than assuming it is personal investment use.

The application would retrieve data through your authorised API, calculate ratios
and valuations, monitor saved company baselines, and analyse financial news locally.
It would retain raw responses, normalized data, provenance and derived HTML/JSON
reports for historical replay. It would also keep local backups and, later, an
encrypted independent recovery copy under the same user's control. No external
AI/model transfer is requested for this initial scope.

Could you identify an appropriate agreement and plan, and confirm:

1. The exact authorised API/endpoints, any exchange or publisher agreements, and
   whether automated retrieval and these local analytical uses are covered.
2. Whether raw responses, news headlines/summaries, derived reports and backups
   may be retained; applicable durations, copy limits, attribution and deletion
   duties, including what must happen on subscription cancellation. Please address
   historical reports and recovery snapshots separately.
3. Coverage for ASML on Euronext Amsterdam, Stadler Rail on SIX in CHF and NVIDIA
   in the US: annual income/cash-flow/balance-sheet data with periods, currencies
   and units; historical prices and adjustment basis; shares, debt and cash;
   dated consensus revenue/EPS estimates, analyst counts and price targets.
4. Availability and separate rights for ASM/BESI/Lam Research peer data, the AEX
   and S&P 500 price-return benchmark series, earnings dates, and company news
   headlines/summaries with publisher URLs and publication timestamps. Please
   identify any gaps, indirect/indicative prices or additional source restrictions.
5. Whether an authorised sample is available for the three primary listings,
   and the total plan/add-on/exchange cost, applicable limits and minimum term.

Please point to the governing provisions or supply written scope confirmation.
If a feature requires separate permission, please identify it rather than treating
all datasets as one entitlement. This enquiry is not a subscription order or an
agreement to charges.

Thank you.
