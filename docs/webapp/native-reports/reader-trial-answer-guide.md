# Native reports — reviewer answer guide

Use after the [reader trial](reader-trial.md). These are expected interpretations
of the controlled synthetic fixtures, **not completed reviewer answers**, verified
company facts or financial approval. Values apply to the named prepared cases;
use the saved report when a fresh fixture differs.

| Task | Expected interpretation and evidence to inspect |
| --- | --- |
| R1 | The saved annual financial period is 31 December 2025 and the inputs are synthetic. Annual revenue is EUR 32.6673bn, with saved prior-year growth about 15.58%. The 9% discount rate, 12% initial growth, 2.5% terminal growth, ten-year horizon and assigned scenario weights need review. The conditional per-share valuation is Keystone's model output. Provider consensus is unavailable; named-broker reports and buy/hold/sell ratings were not collected. See performance, valuation assumptions, Market consensus and External research. |
| R2 | The baseline example separates four original-cycle rows, one original-cycle breach and two surfaced items from five retained export-state rows and two non-retired breaches. An older Delta observation can remain an ongoing breach without being newly surfaced. Policy thresholds are analyst policies, not authenticated covenant terms. Saved local decision attribution is not authenticated financial approval. See cycle/export cards, policy table, dates and correction evidence. |
| R3 | Eight historical cases from eight issuers describe the retained sample; a mean CAR is a historical diagnostic under its saved benchmark/window assumptions. It is not a next-event forecast, causal conclusion or publication approval. Count alone does not establish independent comparable cases, verified timing/provenance or valid inference/control assumptions. The ten-case fixture remains held for other unresolved requirements. The event-relative path is withheld. See sample, case table, interpretation and review requirements. |
| R4 | Inspect retained headline cards and their text-scope labels; headline-only or truncated evidence does not establish full-article content. Published document tone is withheld. A numeric diagnostic zero does not publish a neutral conclusion or a trade signal. Retrieval failure with no usable result differs from retained records whose scorer failed; neither becomes a successful empty/neutral result. See coverage, text scope, excluded/duplicate evidence and diagnostic restrictions. |
| R5 | The default profit bridge is EUR 60 + EUR 10 − EUR 5 = EUR 65. A cost account's own variance of +5 can have an adverse profit impact of −5. The conditional year-end landing of 780 is not guaranteed, calibrated or financially approved. An earlier January correction changes the landing to 800 while June actual remains 65; the submitted delta and effective panel have different scopes. In a closed year the remaining forecast horizon is zero and outcomes are closed-year values, not uncertain future periods. See exact bridge endpoints, account review, forecast basis and source-version panels. |

A satisfactory answer preserves attribution, dates and material restrictions,
even if it uses different words. Record actual confusion before explaining an
answer. Correcting an answer with this guide does not establish an unassisted pass.
All trial and physical/PDF acceptance statuses remain pending until a reviewer
records their own evidence.
